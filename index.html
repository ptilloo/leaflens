🔬 The Science: Why a Leaf Can Reveal a Plant's Health

Hypothesis: the health of a leaf reflects the health of the whole plant. Leaves are the plant's food factories. Their green chlorophyll captures sunlight for photosynthesis, and tiny pores called stomata handle gas exchange and water loss. Because almost every problem a plant faces eventually affects these jobs, it shows up on the leaves first.

  • Pale / yellow leaves (chlorosis) → lack of nutrients like nitrogen, magnesium or iron, so less chlorophyll
  • Dry, brown or wilting leaves → water stress or damaged roots that can't supply water
  • Spots, lesions or mould → fungal, bacterial or viral disease spreading through the plant
  • Holes and bite marks → insect pests eating away the plant's energy-making surface

Scientists use the same idea at a huge scale: satellites measure leaf "greenness" (an index called NDVI) to monitor the health of entire forests and farms. LeafLens applies this principle with AI, reading one leaf to judge the health of the plant.
